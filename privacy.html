Privacy Policy

Applies to Noto for iPad. Last updated 14 September 2026.

The short version

Noto has no account system, no servers of its own, and no analytics. Your notes are files on your iPad and are never uploaded anywhere. The one time Noto uses the network is to read a calendar feed you added yourself.

What Noto stores

Your notes, their pages, any PDFs and images you import, and your settings — all inside Noto’s own container on this device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Noto collects

Nothing. There is no usage tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no third-party code of any kind in this app — it is built entirely with Apple’s own frameworks.

That is a statement about the app. If you choose to contact us, what you send reaches a person — see Getting help below.

The one network request Noto makes

If you add a calendar feed — a Canvas feed link, a Google Calendar iCal address, or any other .ics URL — Noto downloads that address over HTTPS to show your events beside your notes, and refreshes it periodically while you keep it. The request goes to the address you typed and nowhere else, it carries nothing about you or your notes, and the events are cached on this device. Remove the feed and Noto stops making the request. Add no feed and Noto never touches the network at all.

Permissions Noto may ask for

Your device’s Calendar, only if you turn on the calendar strip, and only to read events to show beside your notes — that is a local read, separate from the feed above. The microphone, only when you start a recording inside a note; recordings are saved with that note on this device. Noto never asks for either until you use the feature, and declining leaves the rest of the app working.

Backups and things you send out yourself

Backup, export and share are yours to aim. When you export a PDF or save a backup, it goes wherever you choose — Files, iCloud Drive, another app — and from that point it is covered by that destination’s terms, not this one.

Getting help

If you email support or post in the Noto Discord, we see what you send: your email address or Discord name, and whatever you describe. That is correspondence, not something the app collected — it is never linked to your notes, because Noto has no way to identify them and never sends them anywhere.

For a problem with a purchase we may ask for an App Store order ID, because that is what Apple needs to look it up. Do not send passwords; nobody here will ever ask for one.

Email and Discord each have their own privacy terms, which cover their side of the conversation. Ask us to delete a support thread and we will.

Purchases

Noto Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells this app only whether the purchase is active. Noto never sees your payment details.

Children

Noto collects nothing from anyone, so there is nothing for it to collect from a child either. It shows no ads and has no social or messaging features.

Changes

If this ever stops being true, it will change in an app update and this page will change with it in the same release.
