Noto Support

Noto is a handwritten-notes app for iPad and Apple Pencil.

Current version: 1.0  ·  Contact: open a support request

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in is optional and only needed to buy Noto Plus. Noto has no servers of its own.

Where are my notes kept?

Inside Noto’s own container on your iPad. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you turn on iCloud sync, which keeps a copy in your own iCloud, never on a server of ours. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the iPad, so take a backup first if you plan to reinstall.

Does Noto sync between my iPads?

Yes, if you turn it on. iCloud sync is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings → Connected Services, and it needs your iPads to use the same Apple Account. Your notes go to your own iCloud: Noto has no server of its own and cannot read them. Don’t Sync on a note or folder keeps it on one iPad, and Noto can hold large uploads until you are on Wi-Fi. If an iPad is signed in to a different Apple Account, sync pauses. Nothing is sent to or fetched from the new account until you choose, and notes that came from the old account stay on this iPad unless you confirm. Sync is not a backup: delete a note on one iPad and it is deleted on the others (it goes to Recently Deleted there), and version history stays on each iPad. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored.

What does Noto Plus include?

Unlimited notes — the five-note limit is lifted — and recording audio while you take notes. Playing back, exporting and backing up recordings you already made stays free, always, including if a subscription lapses.

Does handwriting search work?

It reads printed handwriting reasonably well and cursive poorly; maths notation is not recognised. It runs entirely on your iPad, is free, and only ever adds results — it never replaces an exact match on typed or PDF text.

Import and export

A PDF will not import

Noto needs to be able to open the file as a PDF. If it came from a download that is still in progress, or from a cloud folder that has not finished syncing, open it once in Files first so it is fully downloaded, then import it.

Importing from Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive

Install that service’s own app and sign in there once. It then appears as a location inside every Files picker Noto shows, for both importing and saving backups. Noto does not need its own connection to those services.

An export or print did nothing

Export builds the PDF first, which takes a moment on a long note; the share sheet appears when it is ready. If you cancelled part-way, nothing was written and your note is untouched.

Backups

How do I back up everything?

Settings → Export & Backup → Back Up All Notes, then choose any location in Files — including Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or iCloud Drive if you have their apps installed. The backup contains every notebook, folder, imported PDF, image and recording.

What happens when I restore?

Restoring never overwrites an existing notebook. Anything that has changed is kept as a separate copy, so a restore can never silently replace work you did after the backup was taken.

How often should I back up?

Before reinstalling the app, before handing the iPad on, and at the end of a term. Noto saves continuously while you write, but without a backup or iCloud sync the only copy is on the iPad, and deleting the app deletes it. Sync is not a backup: a note you delete on one iPad is deleted on your others.

Something else

Open a support request and include your iPad model, iPadOS version, and what you were doing. If it involves a specific note, say whether it still opens.
